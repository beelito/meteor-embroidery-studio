Questions about Meteor

Which embroidery machines does Meteor work with?

Meteor saves Brother PES (version 6, and version 1 for older software) and Tajima DST. PES opens on Brother and Baby Lock home machines; DST opens on most commercial and multi-needle machines. Pick your Brother machine in Meteor and the hoops and the design check follow its limits, such as the field size, stitch count and color changes.

Can I open designs I already have?

Yes. Open Meteor design files (.meteor), PES and DST embroidery files, and SVGs. Ink/Stitch SVGs keep their supported stitch settings; other SVG artwork can be used as a starting point for digitizing.

Can Meteor digitize a picture for me?

Three ways. Auto-digitize turns a whole picture into stitches in one step, guided digitizing walks you through it piece by piece, and the magic wand sews one color patch at a time where you click. All of them give you shapes with editable curve nodes.

Can I use my own fonts?

Yes. Pick any Google Font from the font browser, or add a TrueType (.ttf) or OpenType (.otf) file. Meteor traces each letter into satin strokes and sews them in the order with the fewest jumps and trims.

Does Meteor run on a Mac?

Yes, on macOS 10.15 or later, with Apple silicon or Intel. Open the disk image and drag Meteor to Applications. The beta is not yet notarized by Apple, so the first time you open it, allow it in System Settings under Privacy & Security. See the 3 steps. iPhone and iPad are not supported yet.

Where are my designs stored?

On your device. Projects save automatically to your computer or tablet. Meteor has no account. Browsing or downloading Google Fonts uses an internet connection; your designs are not uploaded.

What does it cost?

Everything is free during the beta. Pricing for the full release has not been set.

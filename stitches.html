Try it on

Change the stitch. Keep the shape.

Every shape keeps its curve nodes, so you can try a new texture, a new angle or a new stitch type at any time. The stitches redraw as you pick, in real thread.

  • Textures turn a plain fill into wave, brick, diamond or chevron.
  • Turn the fill angle, and the rows follow it.
  • Satin, spiral, concentric and radial fills are one tap away.

Satin corners

Corners that stay crisp.

Where a satin column turns, its stitches would cross and stack on the inside of the bend. Meteor gives every corner its own stitches: they meet on a clean seam, or fan around gentle turns if you prefer.

Mitre: both legs stay square and meet on a seam from the inner corner to a sharp point.

Thread build-up

See where thread piles up.

Extra layers can stiffen fabric and put more stress on the needle. The build-up map shows each square millimetre's layers while you design.

Design check

Fix it before the needle finds it.

Before export, Meteor checks the design against your hoop and machine: size, loose stitches over 7 mm, needle pile-ups, fills too small for tatami, and jumps your machine cannot trim. Most come with a one-click fix.

Privacy

Your designs stay yours.

Last updated November 7, 2026

This website

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other companies: its fonts, images and scripts are all served from this site. It has no forms and asks for nothing.

Like any website, the server that hosts it may keep standard access logs (such as IP address, time and page requested) to keep the site running and secure. These are kept by the hosting provider and are not used to track you.

The Meteor app

Meteor has no account. Browsing and downloading fonts from Google Fonts makes requests to Google’s font services; using local or bundled fonts does not need that connection. Your designs are not uploaded. Your projects, pictures, fonts and exported files are saved only on your own computer or tablet. If the app crashes, it writes a crash log to the same device; it is never sent anywhere.

Changes

If this changes, for example when paid licenses arrive, this page will say what is collected and why before it happens.
